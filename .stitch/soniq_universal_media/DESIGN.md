## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a hyper-clean, futuristic, and luminous aesthetic tailored for audiophiles, cinephiles, and media purists on modern Android hardware. It moves away from the dark, cavernous norms of contemporary media players, asserting precision and technical mastery through an ultra-crisp, light-first canvas.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Acoustic Clarity:** Pristine whites and translucent surfaces evoke a lab-grade, low-distortion audio visualizer.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Electric neon and prismatic gradients inject kinetic life into physical sound waves, progress indicators, and playback controls.
- **Architectural Rigor:** Geometric structure, tabular numeric telemetry, and precise pill badges anchor the user in high-fidelity control.

### Design Movement
A fusion of **Neo-Modernist Luminosity** and **Frosted Precision**:
- Base surfaces leverage crisp gallery whites and ethereal structural tints.
- Interactive mechanisms rely on layered translucent acrylics, razor-thin luminous borders, and concentrated vibrant spectral gradients.
- Micro-surfaces employ ambient shadows tinted with soft atmospheric cool tones to replicate floating optical glass panels over fluid content.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a luminous, ultra-pure base offset by ultra-dense typography and vivid chromatic accents. Accents are applied intentionally to signify live media execution, lossless data fidelity, and precise user interaction.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary canvas and floating cards)
- **Canvas Muted:** `#F8F9FD` (Main scaffolding and system bar underlays)
- **Surface Variant:** `#F0F3F8` (Recessed containers, track grooves, inactive scrub tracks)

### Text & Icon Contrast
- **Text Primary:** `#0D1322` (Headers, track titles, active metrics)
- **Text Secondary:** `#1E293B` (Artist names, album metadata, section headers)
- **Text Tertiary:** `#334155` (Playback durations, bitrates, audio channel counts)
- **Text Muted:** `#64748B` (Inactive controls, secondary timestamps, folder paths)

### Kinetic Accents & Gradients
- **Primary Electric Blue:** `#0066FF` with luminous split to `#00D2FF` (Primary actions, play state, scrubber head).
- **Secondary Violet:** `#7928CA` transitioning to `#8B5CF6` (EQ bands, spatial audio processing, playlist clusters).
- **Tertiary Punch Magenta:** `#FF0080` to `#EC4899` (Hi-Res Lossless, DSD, Master quality tags, peak audio meters).
- **Sonic Flow Linear Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #0066FF 0%, #7928CA 50%, #FF0080 100%)` for hero play triggers, active scrubber fills, and dynamic waveforms.
- **Frosted Border Line:** `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.06)` for regular state; `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.20)` for active media state.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric proportions, low contrast, and high legibility at micro sizes. 

### Numeric & Telemetry Rules
- **Tabular Numerals (`tnum`):** All duration counters, frequency markers (e.g., `32Hz`, `16kHz`), bitrates (`9216 kbps`), and volume decibels must enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- **Badge Casing:** Labels such as `label-sm` and `label-md` utilized within technical badges (FLAC, DSD, 96kHz/24bit) must always render in uppercase with positive letter spacing (`0.05em` to `0.06em`).

## Layout & Spacing
Built for Jetpack Compose on an 8pt architectural rhythm, with a strict 4pt sub-grid for badges, scrubbing offsets, and micro icon-to-label alignment.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile Handset (<600dp):** Single column fluid canvas, 16dp (`margin`) page gutters, 16dp inter-component gutters.
- **Foldables & Tablets (600dp–840dp):** 8-column layout with 24dp margins. Split-screen master-detail views (Queue on left, full player on right).
- **Expanded/Landscape (>840dp):** 12-column layout with persistent rail navigation (80dp width) and docked 320dp playback inspect drawer.

### Insets & Edge-to-Edge
- Content scrolls underneath transparent system bars (StatusBar and NavigationBar).
- Floating components (Mini-player, Floating Bottom Bar) maintain a baseline spacing of `space-md` (16dp) above the system gesture navigation line.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the light theme is created through layered, translucent planes, ultra-fine boundary definition, and multi-layered ambient light diffusion.

### Elevation Levels

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8F9FD` background with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Tonal Surface / Track Rows):** `#FFFFFF` with no shadow, bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards / Media Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` with a dual ambient shadow:
  - Ambient: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.04)`
  - Direct: `0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.02)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.05)`
- **Level 3 (Floating Mini-Player / Sheets):** Frosted glass composition:
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.86)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
  - Shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.03)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)` top edge, `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.06)` perimeter.
- **Level 4 (Hero Action Trigger / Floating Play Button):** Gradient filled with an outer chromatic glow:
  - Base: Linear gradient (`#0066FF` to `#7928CA`)
  - Glow Shadow: `0px 8px 24px -2px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.35), 0px 2px 6px 0px rgba(121, 40, 202, 0.20)`
  - Border: None.

## Shapes
Rounded geometries emphasize tactile modernity and align with Material 3 guidelines.

### Corner Token Mapping
- **Pill (Fully Rounded / 9999px):** Playback scrubbers, status chips, audio format badges, and primary action FABs.
- **Rounded-3xl (24px / 1.5rem):** Floating mini-player container, bottom sheets, context modals, and primary hero album artwork.
- **Rounded-2xl (16px / 1rem):** Media queue cards, equalizer envelope panels, and category tiles.
- **Rounded-lg (12px / 0.75rem):** Thumbnail artwork, track list row selection bounds.
- **Rounded-sm (6px / 0.375rem):** Micro badges and seek thumbnail previews.

## Components

### 1. Primary Action & Play Buttons
- **Hero Play/Pause:** 64dp circular container featuring the Sonic Flow gradient (`#0066FF` through `#7928CA`). Center icon rendered in solid white (`#FFFFFF`, 28dp). Elevation Level 4 with chromatic glow.
- **Secondary Controls:** 44dp circular buttons on `rgba(240, 243, 248, 0.70)` surfaces with `#1E293B` icons. Hover/pressed states shift surface to `#0066FF` at 10% opacity.

### 2. Audio & Video Badges (Lossless / Hi-Res / Formats)
- **Geometry:** Height 22dp, border radius 9999px, horizontal padding 8dp.
- **Styling:** Frosted white background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`), bounded by a 1px stroke using a 50% opacity gradient of `#0066FF` to `#FF0080`.
- **Text:** Font `label-sm` (`10px`, bold, uppercase) filled with a clipped linear gradient (`#0066FF` to `#FF0080`) or deep `#0D1322` for standard codecs (e.g., `AAC`, `MP3`).

### 3. Floating Mini-Player Bar
- **Form:** Docked 16dp above the navigation bar or screen bottom, spanning canvas width minus 32dp. Height 68dp, radius 24dp.
- **Material:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and Level 3 elevation.
- **Layout:** 48dp rounded-lg album art on the left, vertical metadata stack (Title: `title-sm`, Artist: `body-sm`) in the middle, and inline play/pause and queue skip triggers on the right.
- **Progress Track:** 2dp thin line running seamlessly across the bottom border of the mini-player, filled with `#0066FF`.

### 4. Interactive Scrubber & Waveform Seekers
- **Waveform Display:** Vertical bars (2dp width, 1.5dp spacing) rendered in `#F0F3F8` for unplayed regions; played regions render in an interpolated horizontal gradient from `#0066FF` to `#FF0080`.
- **Scrubber Bar:** 4dp track height, expanding to 6dp on touch. Scrubber thumb is a 14dp circle (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `rgba(13, 19, 34, 0.08)` and a central 6dp `#0066FF` dot, casting an ambient shadow (`0px 2px 8px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.35)`).

### 5. Media List Rows
- **Dimensions:** 72dp single row height for high-density audio browsing.
- **States:**
  - Default: `#FFFFFF` background.
  - Active/Playing: `#F8F9FD` background with a 3dp left pill indicator in `#0066FF`. Track title changes from `#0D1322` to `#0066FF`.
- **Metadata Layout:** Left artwork (48dp x 48dp, rounded-lg), center column (Title `title-md`, Subtitle `body-sm` with codec tag), right column (duration in `telemetry-num` and more options trigger).

### 6. Chips & Filtering System
- **Filter Chips:** 36dp pill containers.
  - Inactive: Background `#F0F3F8`, text `#334155`, font `label-lg`, no border.
  - Active: Background `#0D1322`, text `#FFFFFF`, shadow Level 1.

### 7. Form Controls (Switches, Radios, Checkboxes)
- **Switches:** 52dp width, 32dp height pill track. Inactive track is `#F0F3F8` with a 24dp `#FFFFFF` thumb. Active track fills with `#0066FF` and shifts the thumb with an elastic ease curve.
- **Equalizer Sliders:** Vertical 6dp rounded pill groove in `#F0F3F8`. Active fill is `#7928CA` with an oversized 24dp pill thumb displaying current decibel offset in `label-sm`.
