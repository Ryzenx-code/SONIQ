## Brand & Style
The design system adopts an immersive, futuristic dark aesthetic engineered specifically for high-fidelity audio visualizers, media libraries, and cinematic playback on modern OLED hardware. Moving away from muted neutrals, it embraces deep, true-black foundations energized by hyper-luminescent neon accents and ethereal glassmorphic layers.

### Emotional Response & Personality
- **Acoustic Precision:** Inky true-black voids eliminate visual noise, letting media visualizers, audio telemetry, and album artwork take center stage.
- **Electric Vitality:** Glowing neon blue, violet, and hot magenta accents evoke high-voltage synthesizer hardware, fiber-optic data transfer, and festival stage lighting.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered smoked glass, soft glowing rims, and dark slate containers provide a tactile, multidimensional console experience.

### Design Movement
A fusion of **Dark OLED Glassmorphism** and **Cyber-Tactile Futurism**:
- Deep obsidian and midnight slate foundations preserve battery efficiency while maintaining extreme contrast.
- Translucent frosted glass planes with subtle specular highlights float above glowing ambient lighting.
- Interactive controls leverage vivid chromatic linear gradients, luminous backdrops, and crisp typographic telemetry.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated around an ultra-deep OLED dark continuum anchored by `#07090E`, paired with vibrant chromatic neon highlights and crisp, high-contrast text.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas True Black (`#07090E`):** Root background for video viewports, primary scaffolding, and system borders.
- **Canvas Dark Navy (`#0B0F17`):** Base background for scrollable feeds, media drawers, and modal backdrops.
- **Surface Slate Base (`#131926`):** Grounded containers, unselected track rows, and scrubber tracks.
- **Surface Slate Elevated (`#1C2333`):** Active card layers, menu flyouts, dynamic dialogs, and elevated chips.
- **Translucent Glass (`rgba(19, 25, 38, 0.72)`): Frosted panels with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.

### Typography & Visual Contrast
- **Text Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast headers, active track titles, current time stamps, and critical telemetry.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Artist descriptions, album subtitles, playback bitrates, and technical tags.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Inactive indicators, time totals, and divider annotations.

### Chromatic Accents & Kinetic Gradients
- **Primary Electric Blue (`#0066FF` / `#2979FF`):** Active transport triggers, play states, progress thumbs, and selection borders.
- **Secondary Violet (`#A855F7`):** Multi-band EQ nodes, audio routing, playlist tags, and spatial effects.
- **Tertiary Vibrant Magenta (`#EC4899`):** Lossless Master badges, peak meter clips, and dynamic frequency spikes.
- **Sonic Flow Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #0066FF 0%, #A855F7 50%, #EC4899 100%)` for primary play buttons, live visualizer waveforms, and hero progress fills.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic foundation, offering structural clarity, contemporary geometry, and exceptional legibility against deep dark substrates.

### Numeric & Telemetry Rules
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** Timers, scrub positions, frequency spans (`24-bit / 192kHz`), buffer rates, and volume decibel levels must strictly render with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to prevent horizontal jitter during playback.
- **Micro-Badges & Codec Pill Tags:** Codec classifications (e.g., `FLAC`, `DSD512`, `MQA`, `DOLBY ATMOS`) use `label-sm` or `label-md` in all caps with extended tracking (`0.05em` to `0.06em`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates on an 8pt structural rhythm accompanied by a 4pt micro-grid for fine adjustments such as audio meter ticks, seek indicators, and badge padding.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Compact Mobile (<600dp):** Fluid single-column layout. 16dp outer margins, 16dp inter-component gutters.
- **Medium / Tablets (600dp–840dp):** 8-column layout. 24dp outer margins. Supports side-by-side playlist queue and full-size vinyl/cover artwork.
- **Expanded Desktop / In-Car / TV (>840dp):** 12-column layout. 32dp outer canvas margins with persistent 80dp slim navigation rail and docked visualizer console.

### Edge-to-Edge Navigation
Surfaces extend fully behind the OS status and navigation bars. Floating playback controls maintain at least 16dp (`space-md`) vertical separation from gesture bars to prevent interaction conflicts.

## Elevation & Depth
In this dark theme, elevation shifts from casting opaque drop shadows to emitting subtle ambient glows, shifting container slate tones, and employing translucent frosted glass.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Root Void):** Pure `#07090E` base canvas without elevation or borders.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Surfaces & Grid Tiles):** `#0B0F17` or `#131926` filled containers bordered with a 1px hairline stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Media Cards):** Surface `#1C2333` accompanied by low-opacity deep occlusion:
  - Shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.70), 0px 2px 6px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.50)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Level 3 (Floating Mini-Player & Bottom Sheets):** Smoked glass composition:
  - Surface: `rgba(19, 25, 38, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`
  - Shadow: `0px 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0px 0px 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`
  - Rim highlight: 1px top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`
- **Level 4 (Hero Play Triggers & Luminescent Controls):** Gradient filled with chromatic radiance:
  - Base: Sonic Flow Gradient (`#0066FF` through `#EC4899`)
  - Glow: `0px 0px 28px 2px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.45), 0px 0px 14px 0px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.35)`

## Shapes
The design uses rounded-2xl geometry across main interactive units to deliver a smooth tactile character, contrasted by pure pill geometries on micro badges and controls.

### Shape Mapping
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Play/pause buttons, scrub thumbs, filter tags, audio format pills, and volume sliders.
- **Rounded-2xl (16px / `1rem` base roundedness):** Mini-player shell, queue cards, album cards, dialogs, and EQ preset sheets.
- **Rounded-lg (12px / `0.75rem`):** Track row thumbnails, menu popovers, and quick-setting tiles.
- **Rounded-sm (4px / `0.25rem`):** Telemetry tags and mini scrub bar heads.

## Components

### Buttons & Transport Triggers
- **Hero Play/Pause:** 64dp circular control featuring the Sonic Flow gradient (`#0066FF` to `#EC4899`) with pure white icon (`#FFFFFF`). Casts Level 4 chromatic glow.
- **Secondary Transport (Skip/Shuffle/Repeat):** 44dp circular buttons on `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` translucent slate. Icons rendered in `#94A3B8`, shifting to `#2979FF` with a 4dp radiant glow when active.

### Media Cards & Queue Lists
- **Media List Row:** 72dp height. Default state features transparent background with 1px bottom border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`. 
  - Active playing state triggers a background tint of `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.12)`, a 3dp glowing neon electric blue left accent indicator, and shifts the track title to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Album & Video Cards:** 16px corner radius (`rounded-2xl`), nested in `#131926` with a 1px boundary stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover or focus triggers a subtle ambient electric blue halo.

### Floating Mini-Player
- **Container:** Floats 16dp above the lower safe margin. Height 68dp, radius 16px (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Material:** Dark glassmorphic slate (`rgba(19, 25, 38, 0.82)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`.
- **Progress Edge:** A 2dp line at the container's bottom edge with a `#0066FF` to `#A855F7` linear gradient fill denoting playback progress.

### Interactive Seekers & Waveforms
- **Waveform Seeker:** 2dp vertical lines with 1.5dp gaps. Unplayed audio renders in `#1C2333`; played duration illuminates in a vibrant gradient from `#0066FF` through `#EC4899`.
- **Scrubber Bar:** 4dp track in `#1C2333`. Scrubber head is a 14dp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) disc with a `#0066FF` glowing center dot and an active cyan-blue bloom (`0px 0px 10px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.80)`).

### Badges & Technical Telemetry
- **Lossless & Codec Badges:** 22dp height, full pill radius, horizontal padding 8dp. Backed by `rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.12)` with a 1px stroke of `rgba(41, 121, 255, 0.40)`. Text rendered in `label-sm` bold uppercase `#2979FF`.
- **Master Quality / DSD:** Border illuminated in vibrant magenta `rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.50)` with text in `#EC4899`.

### Form Controls & Sliders
- **Switches:** 52dp x 32dp pill track. Unchecked state is `#1C2333` with `#94A3B8` thumb. Checked state fills track with `#0066FF` and renders a pure white thumb with subtle electric blue ambient glow.
- **Sliders & Equalizer Knobs:** 6dp vertical or horizontal rounded channels in `#131926`. Active filled spans illuminate in `#A855F7`.
